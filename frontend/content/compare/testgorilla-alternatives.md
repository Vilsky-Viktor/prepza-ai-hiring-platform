# TestGorilla alternatives: where each tool fits, and using them together

TestGorilla is a skills testing platform with a large test library and a free plan ([TestGorilla pricing page](https://www.testgorilla.com/pricing/)). Teams look at other tools for a few common reasons: an annual commitment doesn't fit occasional hiring, they want hands-on coding tests, or they want tests written for their exact role.

Most of these tools aren't direct substitutes for each other. They test different things, and many hiring processes use two of them together. testee in particular is mostly complementary: it adds a quick knowledge screen, written from your job description, in front of whatever else you use. This page explains where each tool fits and how they combine. Facts link to each vendor's own pages as of October 2026; check them before you buy.

## How to pick

Start with what you need to test:

- **Role knowledge for any job** (sales, accounting, support, operations, marketing, engineering, including code-reading questions): a tool that builds tests for your role. testee fits here.
- **Hands-on coding** (writing and running code): a developer-focused platform such as HackerRank, CodeSignal or Codility.
- **Data and analytics roles:** Alooba focuses there.
- **Cognitive ability and personality:** a psychometric vendor, or Bryq, or TestGorilla itself.

Then check the pricing model against your hiring volume. A plan suits steady hiring; pay-per-candidate suits occasional hiring. If you need more than one kind of test, you may end up with two tools, each doing what it's built for.

## The tools in more detail

### testee

testee turns a job description into a timed multiple-choice skills interview. You review the proposed topics before any question is written, and each candidate gets their own random set of questions with a countdown on each one. For technical roles, questions can show a short code example and ask what it does or outputs. Scorecards show every answer and its timing, with flags for too-fast answers, leaving the page and copy attempts. You invite by email or with one link in your job ad, and reminders go out automatically.

- **Pricing:** per candidate who answers at least one question, cheaper per candidate on larger top-ups. Credits don't expire, and there's no subscription. See [pricing](/pricing).
- **Suits:** small and mid-size teams hiring for any role, a few people at a time, as a first screen.
- **Works with your tools:** Workable, Greenhouse, Teamtailor, Recruitee and Breezy HR send candidates in and get their results back, Slack hears when candidates finish, and a public API connects your own platform. All at no extra cost.
- **Not for:** cognitive or personality tests, writing or running code, or video interviews. testee doesn't offer them, so pair it with a tool that does when you need them.

### CodeSignal

CodeSignal's plans cover technical assessments and AI interviewers for product, design and engineering roles, with AI proctoring and ID verification. Higher plans add go-to-market assessments and AI interviewers for sales, customer success and marketing, plus ATS integrations ([CodeSignal pricing page, as of October 2026](https://codesignal.com/pricing/)).

- **Better than testee for:** hands-on coding assessment, AI-led interviews.
- **Together:** testee as a knowledge screen for every applicant, CodeSignal for the coding stage.

### HackerRank

HackerRank focuses on developer hiring. As of October 2026, its site describes screening tests, live pair-programming interviews and an AI interviewer ([hackerrank.com](https://www.hackerrank.com/)).

- **Better than testee for:** developer hiring where you need code to be written and run, or live coding interviews.
- **Together:** see [HackerRank alternatives](/compare/hackerrank-alternatives) for how a knowledge screen and a developer platform fit in one process.

### Alooba

Alooba lists 400+ role screening tests across 500+ skills, including data analysis tests. Higher plans add proctoring, ATS integrations, SMS reminders and video answers ([Alooba pricing page, as of October 2026](https://www.alooba.com/pricing/)).

- **Better than testee for:** data and analytics roles that need hands-on data tests, proctoring and video answers.

### Bryq

Bryq's plans include cognitive and personality assessments, skills assessments, unlimited invitations, an AI job builder, ATS integration and PDF results; culture fit is listed as an add-on ([Bryq pricing page, as of October 2026](https://www.bryq.com/pricing)).

- **Better than testee for:** cognitive and personality profiling, culture fit, and unlimited invitations at a flat price.
- **Together:** testee checks role knowledge; Bryq adds cognitive and personality results.

### Psychometric specialists: Criteria, Wonderlic, SHL

These vendors focus on cognitive and personality testing. If psychometrics are central to how you hire, they are likely a better fit than testee for that part, and testee can still cover role knowledge.

## Where TestGorilla is still the better fit

TestGorilla may suit you better if:

- You use several test types together: role skills, cognitive, personality, language.
- You want a free plan for very light use ([pricing page](https://www.testgorilla.com/pricing/)).
- You need video questions, job simulations or webcam snapshots.
- You hire at steady, high volume, where a flat plan costs less per candidate.

And it can still sit next to testee: a role-specific knowledge screen first, TestGorilla's broader tests later. For a one-to-one look, read [testee and TestGorilla](/compare/testgorilla).

## Using tools together

A simple combined process:

1. **Screen knowledge for everyone** with a short test written from the job description.
2. **Add the specialist test** for the short list: coding, cognitive, personality or data, depending on the role.
3. **Interview** the finalists with set questions and a scoring guide.

What each tool costs depends on your volume and the plans you need. [How to compare test costs](/compare/cheapest-pre-employment-test) shows how to estimate it from each vendor's pricing page.
