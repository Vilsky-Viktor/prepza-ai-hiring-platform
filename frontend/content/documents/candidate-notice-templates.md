# Candidate notice templates

Version of 2026-10-17. Arcolabs OÜ (registry code 17587452), Sepapaja tn 6, 15551 Tallinn, Estonia; hello@prepza.ai.

For companies to adapt and send **before** inviting candidates to a prepza interview. Replace
everything in [brackets]. The notice is yours to give as the employer: these templates cover what
prepza does, and you add how you use the results. In the EU, telling candidates that a high-risk
AI system is used to assess them is one of your duties under the AI Act from 2027-12-02; the
general notice (section 2) does it.

## 1. New York City (Local Law 144, automated employment decision tool)

Local Law 144 applies when an employer or employment agency uses an automated employment decision
tool to substantially assist decisions about candidates who live in New York City. Whether prepza is
such a tool depends on how you use it. The rules require, among other things: a bias audit within
one year before use with a public summary; a notice at least **10 business days** before use (in
the job posting, on the careers site, or by mail or email); and information on how to request an
alternative selection process or accommodation.

Under Local Law 144 the employer or employment agency commissions the bias audit and gives the
notice: prepza doesn't do it for you. prepza gives you the candidates' results for your auditor
(API, PDF reports), the summary of its own independent bias audit once published at
prepza.ai/documents, and this template. Don't use prepza for candidates in New York City before
your audit summary is published (see prepza's instructions for companies).

> **Notice of use of an automated employment decision tool**
>
> [Company] uses **prepza**, an automated employment decision tool, to help assess candidates for
> the position of [job title]. We are giving you this notice at least 10 business days before you
> will be assessed.
>
> **What it assesses.** A timed multiple-choice test of job knowledge in these areas, taken from
> the job's requirements: [list topics]. Each question has a time limit of [N] seconds. Your score
> is the percentage of points earned: each question is worth one point, and on a "select all that
> apply" question you earn part of it for each right option ticked, and nothing if you tick a
> wrong one. The tool also records how long each answer
> took, when you left the test page or copied text during it, and answers given too fast to have
> read the question. Before you start, you confirm that you'll answer on your own, without AI tools
> or other people, and when you did is recorded. [Company] may give you extra time, which is
> recorded without a reason.
>
> **How it is used.** Your score is compared with a passing grade of [N]% and with other
> candidates' scores. [Name or role at Company] reviews every result before any decision, together
> with [your application / interviews]. The tool does not make the hiring decision.
>
> **Data.** The tool uses your email address, your name (from your application or your sign-in),
> the account id from your sign-in, and your answers in the test, with how long each took, page
> leaves, copy attempts, answers too fast to have read the question, when you confirmed answering
> on your own, and any extra time you were given. The questions were written with AI from the job description,
> on topics [Company] chose. Your test data is kept by prepza for 12 months after your invitation, then
> deleted. [Company's own retention policy: ...]. More information about the data collected, its
> source and retention policy is available on request at [contact], within 30 days of a written
> request.
>
> **Bias audit.** A summary of the most recent bias audit of this tool is available at [link to
> your bias audit summary].
>
> **Alternative process or accommodation.** You may request an alternative selection process or an
> accommodation (for example extra time) by writing to [contact] by [date]. [Describe the
> alternative, e.g. a structured interview.] Requesting one will not count against you.
>
> **Human review.** You can ask [Company] for a human review of your result at [contact].

## 2. General candidate notice (EU, UK and US)

> **About the [job title] assessment**
>
> As part of your application to [Company], you're invited to take a short timed knowledge test on
> **prepza**. Please read this before you start.
>
> **What it is.** An AI system that helps us assess candidates; under the EU AI Act it counts as a
> high-risk AI system. [N] multiple-choice questions about [topics], with [N] seconds for each.
> Questions were written with the help of AI from the job description, on topics our team chose.
> Each candidate gets their own selection of questions. Most have one right option; a "select all
> that apply" question has 2 or 3, and you tick every one you think is right: it earns part of its
> point for each right option ticked, and nothing if a wrong one is ticked. You can change your pick
> until you move to the next question or finish. When a question's time runs out, your pick
> counts; with no pick, it counts as wrong.
>
> **What is recorded.** Your answers (every option ticked), whether each was right or the points
> it earned, how long each took, when you left the test page or copied text, and answers given
> too fast to have read the question, and when you confirmed, before starting, that you'd answer
> on your own. If we give you extra time, that is recorded too, without a reason. You won't see your score.
>
> **How we use it.** Your result is one part of our assessment, alongside [your CV / interviews].
> A person at [Company] reviews it; no decision is made automatically. You can ask us for a human
> review of your result and for an explanation of how it was used, at [contact].
>
> **Accommodations.** If you need extra time or another adjustment because of a disability or
> another reason, or would prefer a different way to show your knowledge, write to [contact]
> before you start. We can give you more time on every question or arrange an alternative.
> If you apply through our job ad's link, ask us for a personal invite first: extra time can't be
> added there. You don't need to tell us a diagnosis.
>
> **Your data.** [Company] is responsible for your test data; prepza (Arcolabs OÜ, Estonia)
> processes it for us. It is kept for 12 months after your invitation and then deleted
> automatically. Our legal basis is [legitimate interest in assessing job knowledge fairly / steps
> before entering into a contract]. You can ask us for a copy of your data, its correction or
> deletion, or object, at [contact]. In the EU or UK you can complain to your data protection
> authority. prepza's privacy policy: https://prepza.ai/privacy.
>
> **Emails.** prepza sends your invitation and reminders for us. Each has a link to stop our emails
> through prepza, or that test's reminders.
>
> **Tips.** Use a computer with a stable connection, in a quiet place, and keep the test page open:
> each question's timer keeps running if you leave.
